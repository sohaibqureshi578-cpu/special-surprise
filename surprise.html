Hamari Khoobsurat Kahani

Har kahani ka ek khoobsurat aaghaz hota hai. Kuch mulaqatein aur kuch yaadein waqt ke saath dil ke bohat qareeb ho jati hain.

Sohaib aur Eshmal ke naam se judi ye kahani bhi pyare ehsaas, muskurahaton, izzat aur bharose ka ek khoobsurat safar hai.

Zindagi ka asal husn tab hai jab log ek doosre ko samjhein, ek doosre ki khushi ka khayal rakhein aur mushkil waqt mein bhi achhe alfaaz aur izzat ke saath saath dein.

"Kuch rishte lafzon se nahi, achhe ehsaason se bante hain." ❤

Khaas Lamhe Aur Khoobsurat Ehsaas

Ek pyari si muskurahat, ek achha paigham, ek doosre ki fikr aur chhoti chhoti khushiyan kisi bhi rishte ko khoobsurat bana deti hain.

Dua hai ke Sohaib aur Eshmal ki zindagi mein aise hi bohat se khushgawar lamhe aate rahein.
